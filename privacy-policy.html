Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Recall Education Ltd ("Recall", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data about you when you use our online learning platform at recalleducation.co.uk (the "Service"). It also describes the rights you have over your data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018.

We take data minimisation seriously. We collect only what we need to run the Service, we don't sell your data, and we make it straightforward to export or delete your account.

1. Who we are

For the purposes of data protection law, the data controller is Recall Education Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14882047). Our registered office is in Sheffield, United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach our Data Protection lead at privacy@recalleducation.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Account data

  • Email address, display name, and (optionally) full name.
  • Year group, school, and (if you choose to provide them) date of birth and parent/guardian email.
  • Authentication credentials — handled by our auth provider; we store password hashes, not plain passwords.
  • Profile photo URL if you upload one.

Learning data

  • Lessons attempted, questions answered, scores, time spent, and progress through courses.
  • Spaced-repetition review history (which cards you've seen and when).
  • Notes and bookmarks you choose to save.

Communications data

  • Direct messages you send or receive inside Recall (DMs between users).
  • Notifications we deliver to you and read-receipt state.
  • Support emails you send us and our replies.

Technical data

  • IP address, device type, browser type, and operating system.
  • Pages visited, features used, referring URL, and timestamps. We use this for security, debugging, and aggregated analytics.
  • Authentication session tokens.

Free-text content

  • Content you write in bios, profile names, lesson comments, and DMs.
  • This content is scanned by an automated moderation system. Flagged content is stored for staff review. See section 5 for who can see it.

3. How we use it

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Service. Authenticating you, displaying your dashboard, syncing your progress across devices, delivering notifications, and routing DMs.
  • Personalising learning. Choosing which questions to show you, scheduling reviews at the right time, and adapting explanations to your year group and exam board.
  • Safety and moderation. Detecting and reviewing abuse, fraud, and content that breaches our Terms. See section 5 for the human access involved.
  • Customer support. Responding to questions, diagnosing bugs, and fixing problems you report.
  • Improvement. Understanding which lessons and features are most useful, and where users get stuck. This is done on aggregated or pseudonymised data wherever possible.
  • Billing. Processing subscription payments and refunds through our payment processor.
  • Legal compliance. Complying with applicable law, responding to lawful requests, and enforcing our Terms.

4. Legal basis

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the Service you have signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — for safety, security, fraud prevention, product analytics, and improving Recall. We balance these interests against your rights and freedoms.
  • Consent — for anything optional, such as marketing emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we need to keep records to comply with tax, accounting, or safeguarding law.
  • Vital interests — in rare cases where we need to act to protect someone's life or safety.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the categories of recipient below, and only the minimum needed for them to do their job.

  • Other users. Your public profile (display name, avatar, year group, school, bio) is visible to other signed-in Recall users as part of the platform's social features. Direct messages are visible only to the sender and the named recipient(s).
  • Staff moderators. When free-text content is flagged by our automated system, a small team of authorised moderators (Recall staff with the admin or staff_reviewer role) can review the flagged text and the surrounding context (your profile row and, for DMs, the message and its thread) so they can decide whether to approve or reject it.
  • Service providers (processors). Companies that help us run Recall — for example our hosting provider, database provider, authentication provider, payment processor, email/SMS provider, and analytics provider. Each is bound by a data processing agreement and only receives the data they need.
  • Schools. If you signed up through a school or your parent connected you to one, your school organiser or teacher may see your learning progress and, in some configurations, your free-text activity in class contexts. You can disconnect from a school at any time from your account settings.
  • Authorities. Where we are legally required to disclose data (for example, under a court order or to report safeguarding concerns), or where we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to protect someone's safety.
  • Successors in business. If Recall is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred to the successor entity under the same protections as this policy.

6. How long we keep it

We keep data for as long as you have an account, plus a reasonable period afterwards so you can reactivate if you change your mind. Specifically:

  • Account and learning data — kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or fully anonymise this data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legitimate business or legal reasons (see below).
  • Direct messages — kept while the conversation is active. When you delete your account, your messages are deleted from other users' inboxes.
  • Moderation flags and review notes — kept for 12 months after resolution for safety pattern analysis, then deleted.
  • Billing records — kept for at least 6 years to comply with UK tax law.
  • Backups — operational backups are retained on a rolling 30-day window and may include your data; once a backup falls outside that window it is overwritten.
  • Aggregated analytics — retained indefinitely in a form that does not identify you.

7. International transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside the UK (for example, our database and analytics providers). When we transfer your data internationally, we rely on one of the safeguards recognised by UK GDPR — typically the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or a UK adequacy decision. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by emailing privacy@recalleducation.co.uk.

8. How we protect it

No system is perfectly secure, but we take steps to protect your data:

  • HTTPS everywhere on the Service.
  • Row-level security on our database so users can only access their own data.
  • Encrypted password storage and hashed session tokens.
  • Access to production data by staff is logged, audited, and limited to what each role needs.
  • Regular backup and disaster recovery testing.
  • Incident response procedures — if we ever have a personal data breach that affects your rights, we will notify you and the ICO where required by law.

9. Children

Recall is designed for secondary-school students, including users under 16. We treat under-16 accounts with extra care:

  • We do not show third-party advertising to under-16 accounts, and we do not use behavioural tracking for ad-targeting.
  • If a user is under 16 and signed up directly (rather than through a school), we may ask for a parent/guardian email so that the parent can be informed and consent to the account. We do not market to the parent's email without their consent.
  • We never share an under-16 user's personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • Parents can ask us to delete their child's account at any time by emailing privacy@recalleducation.co.uk.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data in certain circumstances (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability — receive the data you provided to us in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where our legal basis is consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

You can exercise most of these rights yourself from your account settings (export and delete are both available there). For anything else, email privacy@recalleducation.co.uk and we will respond within one calendar month.

11. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar storage, mostly to keep you signed in. Specifically:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — for authentication, security, and remembering your "keep me signed in" choice. These cannot be switched off while using the Service.
  • Preference cookies — to remember small things like your last viewed subject.
  • Analytics cookies — optional. If we enable third-party analytics, we will ask for your consent before setting the cookie and we will use a privacy-respecting tool that does not identify you across sites.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not let third parties set tracking cookies through Recall.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If a change is material, we will give you reasonable notice by email or by a prominent notice in the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect when the changes were made.

13. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy or how we handle your data, please get in touch:

  • Email: privacy@recalleducation.co.uk
  • Post: Recall Education Ltd, Sheffield, United Kingdom

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
