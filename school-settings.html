Register late window

How long after a lesson's scheduled start a register can be submitted before it's flagged LATE.
Between 0 and 240. Registers taken from the lessons list use this window; ad-hoc registers are never flagged. The flag is set on the first submission only — later edits don't change it.

School day

How many periods the day has and when they run. The booking grid and timetables use this structure.
Between 1 and 12. Changing this resizes the timetable and booking grids straight away.
Period 1 starts at this time; each period runs consecutively (breaks aren't modelled).
Informational — periods may end before or after this.

Resource booking

How far ahead staff can book, and for how long. Bookings always fall inside the school day's periods.
0 = no limit. Otherwise a single booking can span at most this many periods.
0 = no limit. Past dates are never allowed either way.

Staff roles

Give staff named roles with specific permissions — like Discord roles.
Staff with no role keep every permission — roles only ever restrict or grant. As the organiser you always have every permission, whatever your roles say.
